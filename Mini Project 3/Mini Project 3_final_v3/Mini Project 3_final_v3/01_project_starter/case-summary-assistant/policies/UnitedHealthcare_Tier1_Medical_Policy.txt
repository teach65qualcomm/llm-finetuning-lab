UnitedHealthcare Tier 1 — Medical Policy and Coverage Rules
Version 2024.1 | Effective January 1, 2024

PAGE 1
SECTION 1: COVERAGE PRINCIPLES
UnitedHealthcare covers medically necessary services when provided by network providers.
Tier 1 members receive the highest benefit level with 90-95% coverage for most services.
Prior authorization decisions are made within 3 business days for standard requests and
same day for URGENT or STAT clinical situations.

PAGE 2
SECTION 2: INFECTIOUS DISEASE TREATMENT
Treatment for infectious disease diagnoses including sepsis (A41.9) and related conditions
requires prior authorization when treatment involves IV antibiotics, hospitalization, or
specialty infectious disease consultation. Standard antibiotic courses for common infections
(A49.01, A02.0) do not require prior authorization for Tier 1 members.

PAGE 3
SECTION 3: ENDOCRINE AND METABOLIC CONDITIONS
Management of diabetes (E11.9, E10.9) including insulin pump therapy, continuous glucose
monitoring, and related procedures requires prior authorization. Lipid management (E78.5)
with PCSK9 inhibitors requires step therapy documentation. Standard diabetes management
supplies and visits are covered without prior authorization.

PAGE 4
SECTION 4: RESPIRATORY PROCEDURES
COPD management (J44.1) including pulmonary rehabilitation requires prior authorization.
Asthma treatment (J45.50) with biologics requires step therapy failure documentation.
Standard respiratory office visits and spirometry do not require prior authorization.
Emergency respiratory care (J96.00) is covered without prior authorization.

PAGE 5
SECTION 5: CLAIM SUBMISSION AND APPEALS
Claims must be submitted within 180 days of service. Members have the right to appeal
any adverse determination within 180 days of the denial notice. Expedited appeals for
urgent clinical situations are reviewed within 72 hours.
