Cigna Medical Tier 2 — Coverage Policy
Version 2024.1 | Effective January 1, 2024

PAGE 1
SECTION 1: TIER 2 MEMBER BENEFITS
Cigna Tier 2 members receive standard network access with moderate cost-sharing requirements.
Prior authorization turnaround: 5 business days for standard, 24 hours for URGENT, 4 hours for STAT.
Tier 2 members have an annual deductible of $1,000 individual and $2,000 family.

PAGE 2
SECTION 2: NEUROLOGICAL PROCEDURES
MRI brain with contrast (CPT 70553) for neurological indications requires prior authorization.
Cigna requires documentation of clinical necessity including symptom duration and prior
conservative treatment. Neurological conditions including migraines (G43.909), multiple
sclerosis (G35), and Parkinson's disease (G20) are covered. Coverage is 80% in-network.

PAGE 3
SECTION 3: MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES
Mental health treatment including depression (F32.1), anxiety (F41.1), and schizophrenia (F20.9)
is covered at parity with medical benefits. Inpatient mental health requires prior authorization.
Outpatient psychotherapy does not require prior authorization for the first 12 sessions.
Bipolar disorder (F31.9) and ADHD (F90.0) treatment plans require an annual review.

PAGE 4
SECTION 4: DIGESTIVE AND GI COVERAGE
Colonoscopy for diagnostic purposes (CPT 45378) requires prior authorization when not
part of standard screening intervals. Upper GI procedures and liver disease management
(K74.60) require documentation of medical necessity. GI bleeds (K92.1) are treated as
urgent and do not require prior authorization.

PAGE 5
SECTION 5: PRIOR AUTHORIZATION DENIAL CRITERIA
Cigna may deny prior authorization when: the procedure is not medically necessary per
evidence-based guidelines, the member has not completed required step therapy,
documentation is incomplete, or the service is experimental/investigational.
