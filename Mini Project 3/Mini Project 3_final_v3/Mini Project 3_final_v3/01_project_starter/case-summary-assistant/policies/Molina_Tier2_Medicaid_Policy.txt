Molina Healthcare Tier 2 — Coverage Policy
Version 2024.1 | Effective January 1, 2024

PAGE 1
SECTION 1: OVERVIEW
Molina Healthcare provides managed care coverage with emphasis on preventive care and
chronic disease management. Prior authorization is required for specialty care, elective
procedures, and high-cost services. Standard review: 3 business days.

PAGE 2
SECTION 2: CHRONIC DISEASE MANAGEMENT
Diabetes management (E11.9, E10.9) is a priority program. Members with diabetes receive
enhanced coverage for glucose monitoring, diabetes education, and annual eye exams without
prior authorization. Insulin pump therapy and continuous glucose monitoring require
prior authorization with documentation of uncontrolled diabetes despite oral medication.

PAGE 3
SECTION 3: ONCOLOGY SERVICES
Cancer diagnosis and treatment is covered with prior authorization. Molina partners with
regional cancer centers for complex oncology cases. Chemotherapy, radiation, and surgical
oncology procedures require prior authorization and treatment plan review by Molina's
oncology clinical team. Coverage is 85% in-network for authorized oncology services.

PAGE 4
SECTION 4: BEHAVIORAL HEALTH
Mental health and substance use disorder services are covered. Inpatient psychiatric
admission requires prior authorization and concurrent review. Outpatient behavioral
health services are covered without prior authorization for the first 6 sessions.
Crisis mental health services are covered without prior authorization.

PAGE 5
SECTION 5: CARE COORDINATION
Molina provides care coordinators for members with chronic conditions. Care managers
review complex prior authorization cases and coordinate between providers. Members may
request care coordination assistance by calling the member services number.
