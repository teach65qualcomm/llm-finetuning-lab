Ambetter Insurance Tier 3 — Health Exchange Policy
Version 2024.1 | Effective January 1, 2024

PAGE 1
SECTION 1: EXCHANGE PLAN COVERAGE
Ambetter Tier 3 is an Affordable Care Act exchange plan with essential health benefits.
All ACA-required services are covered. Prior authorization requirements are strictly
enforced. Standard review: 5 business days. URGENT: 24 hours. STAT: 8 hours.

PAGE 2
SECTION 2: ESSENTIAL HEALTH BENEFITS
The following require prior authorization for Tier 3 members: specialty care referrals,
non-emergency surgical procedures, MRI and CT scans, biologic medications, and mental
health inpatient admission. Emergency care does not require prior authorization but must
be followed up with notification within 24 hours of admission.

PAGE 3
SECTION 3: YOUNG ADULT COVERAGE (18-30)
Members aged 18-30 on Ambetter exchange plans have reduced cost-sharing for preventive
services. Reproductive health services are covered without prior authorization. Mental
health and substance use disorder services are covered at parity. Preventive screenings
appropriate for age group are covered at 100%.

PAGE 4
SECTION 4: STEP THERAPY REQUIREMENTS
Ambetter requires step therapy for: biologic medications (must fail 2 conventional agents),
brand-name medications when generics are available, and specialty orthopedic devices.
Step therapy exemption requests require documentation of previous treatment failure and
provider attestation.

PAGE 5
SECTION 5: COST-SHARING SCHEDULE
Tier 3 cost sharing: Primary care visit $40 copay. Specialist visit $80 copay.
Emergency room $350 copay. Inpatient hospital 30% coinsurance after $2,500 deductible.
Outpatient surgery 30% coinsurance. Prescription drugs: Tier 1 $10, Tier 2 $40, Tier 3 $80.
