Humana Insurance Tier 2 — Authorization Manual
Version 2024.2 | Effective February 1, 2024

PAGE 1
SECTION 1: AUTHORIZATION REQUIREMENTS
Humana Tier 2 requires prior authorization for procedures with high clinical variability
or cost. Standard review: 5 business days. URGENT: 24 hours. STAT: 4 hours.
Authorization is valid for 90 days from the approval date.

PAGE 2
SECTION 2: CARDIOVASCULAR COVERAGE
Echocardiography (CPT 93306) requires prior authorization for Tier 2 members when
ordered for non-acute indications. Coronary artery bypass surgery (CPT 33533) requires
second surgical opinion and prior authorization. Coverage is 80% in-network.
Cardiac rehabilitation programs require prior authorization and are limited to 36 sessions.

PAGE 3
SECTION 3: MUSCULOSKELETAL CONDITIONS
Back pain (M54.5) management including surgery requires documentation of 6 months of
conservative therapy failure. Total knee replacement (CPT 27447) requires physical therapy
documentation and weight management compliance if BMI > 40. Rheumatoid arthritis (M06.9)
biologic therapy requires step therapy with at least 2 conventional DMARDs.

PAGE 4
SECTION 4: GENITOURINARY CONDITIONS
Kidney disease management (N18.3) including dialysis requires prior authorization.
Prostate conditions (N40.0) requiring surgical intervention need prior authorization.
Kidney stones (N20.0) treatment with lithotripsy requires prior authorization.
UTI treatment (N39.0) does not require prior authorization.

PAGE 5
SECTION 5: PEDIATRIC AND YOUNG ADULT COVERAGE
Members under 18 require parental consent for all prior authorization requests.
Pediatric specialty services are covered at 85% in-network. Young adult members
(18-30) on parental coverage follow the same authorization requirements as adult members.
