BlueCross BlueShield Tier 1 — General Coverage Policy
Version 2024.1 | Effective January 1, 2024

PAGE 1
SECTION 1: INTRODUCTION
This policy governs coverage for all Tier 1 BlueCross BlueShield members. Tier 1 members
receive preferred provider network access with the lowest out-of-pocket costs. All
prior-authorization requests for Tier 1 members are processed within 72 hours for
standard requests and 24 hours for urgent or STAT requests.

PAGE 2
SECTION 2: CARDIOVASCULAR PROCEDURES
Cardiovascular diagnostic procedures including echocardiography (CPT 93306), stress testing,
and Holter monitoring are covered at 90% after deductible for Tier 1 members. Prior
authorization is required for all elective cardiovascular procedures. Urgent cardiovascular
cases marked URGENT or STAT receive expedited review and are typically approved within 4 hours.
Coverage applies when the procedure is ordered by a network cardiologist or primary care physician.

PAGE 3
SECTION 3: SURGICAL PROCEDURES
Complex surgical procedures including coronary artery bypass (CPT 33533) and valve repair
require prior authorization and second surgical opinion for elective cases. Emergency
surgical procedures are covered without prior authorization. Tier 1 members receive 95%
coverage for in-network surgical procedures after meeting their annual deductible of $500.

PAGE 4
SECTION 4: IMAGING AND DIAGNOSTICS
MRI with contrast (CPT 70553) is covered for neurological and oncological indications.
Prior authorization required. Chest X-ray (CPT 71046) does not require prior authorization
for Tier 1 members. CT scans require authorization only when part of a cancer staging protocol.

PAGE 5
SECTION 5: MENTAL HEALTH PARITY
Psychotherapy sessions (CPT 90837) are covered at parity with medical benefits. Tier 1
members may access up to 52 sessions per calendar year without prior authorization for
the first 8 sessions. Sessions 9 and beyond require a treatment plan review.
