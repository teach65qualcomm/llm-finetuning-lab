Centene Corporation Tier 3 — Restricted Coverage Policy
Version 2024.1 | Effective January 1, 2024

PAGE 1
SECTION 1: TIER 3 COVERAGE OVERVIEW
Centene Tier 3 provides essential coverage with higher cost-sharing and stricter
prior authorization requirements. Annual deductible: $2,500. Out-of-pocket maximum: $7,000.
Standard prior authorization: 7 business days. URGENT: 48 hours. STAT: 24 hours.

PAGE 2
SECTION 2: HIGH-COMPLEXITY PROCEDURE REQUIREMENTS
Complex surgical procedures including craniotomy (CPT 61510), mastectomy (CPT 19307),
and coronary bypass (CPT 33533) require: (1) prior authorization, (2) second surgical
opinion, (3) peer-to-peer review with Centene medical director, and (4) board-certified
specialist documentation. Coverage is 70% in-network after $2,500 deductible.

PAGE 3
SECTION 3: ONCOLOGY PRIOR AUTHORIZATION
Cancer-related procedures including surgery, chemotherapy, and radiation require prior
authorization. Centene requires NCCN guideline compliance documentation for all oncology
treatment plans. Experimental or off-label treatments require additional medical
necessity review and may be denied if not supported by peer-reviewed evidence.

PAGE 4
SECTION 4: MENTAL HEALTH RESTRICTIONS
Mental health services are covered but subject to Tier 3 cost-sharing. Inpatient
psychiatric care requires prior authorization and is limited to medically necessary stays.
Outpatient psychotherapy requires prior authorization after the initial assessment visit.
Applied behavior analysis (ABA) for autism requires annual prior authorization.

PAGE 5
SECTION 5: APPEALS AND GRIEVANCES
Members have the right to appeal adverse prior authorization decisions within 60 days.
Standard appeal review: 30 days. Expedited appeal (urgent cases): 72 hours. External
independent review is available after exhausting internal appeal process.
