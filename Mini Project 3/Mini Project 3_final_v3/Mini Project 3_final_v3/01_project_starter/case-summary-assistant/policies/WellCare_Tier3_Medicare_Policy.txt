WellCare Health Tier 3 — Medicare Advantage Policy
Version 2024.1 | Effective January 1, 2024

PAGE 1
SECTION 1: MEDICARE ADVANTAGE COVERAGE
WellCare Tier 3 Medicare Advantage plan provides coverage following CMS guidelines.
Prior authorization requirements comply with Medicare coverage determinations.
Standard review: 14 days. URGENT: 72 hours. No STAT category for Medicare Advantage.

PAGE 2
SECTION 2: CARDIOVASCULAR SERVICES FOR OLDER ADULTS
Members over 70 with cardiovascular diagnoses (I25.10, I50.9, I48.91) receive enhanced
monitoring and care coordination. Echocardiography (CPT 93306) requires prior authorization
but is processed within 48 hours for members with documented cardiac history.
Coronary bypass requires hospitalization pre-authorization and peer review.

PAGE 3
SECTION 3: ORTHOPEDIC COVERAGE FOR SENIOR MEMBERS
Total knee replacement (CPT 27447) for members 56-70 requires prior authorization,
functional assessment, and documentation of failed conservative therapy. Members over
70 also require cardiac clearance for major orthopedic procedures. Coverage is 75%
in-network after $2,500 annual deductible.

PAGE 4
SECTION 4: RESPIRATORY DISEASE MANAGEMENT
COPD (J44.1) and respiratory failure (J96.00) management including home oxygen therapy
requires prior authorization. Pulmonary function testing does not require prior authorization.
Pneumonia (J18.9) treatment in older adults is expedited and processed within 24 hours.

PAGE 5
SECTION 5: PREVENTIVE SERVICES
Medicare-covered preventive services including annual wellness visits, screenings, and
vaccines are covered at 100% without prior authorization. Colonoscopy screening (CPT 45378)
at standard intervals does not require prior authorization for Medicare members.
