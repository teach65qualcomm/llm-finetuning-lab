Aetna Health Tier 1 — Prior Authorization Guidelines
Version 2024.2 | Effective March 1, 2024

PAGE 1
SECTION 1: PRIOR AUTHORIZATION OVERVIEW
Aetna requires prior authorization for procedures that are medically complex, high-cost,
or where clinical appropriateness review is needed. Tier 1 members benefit from
streamlined authorization processes with 48-hour turnaround for standard requests.
URGENT requests are reviewed within 8 hours. STAT requests within 2 hours.

PAGE 2
SECTION 2: ONCOLOGY COVERAGE
Cancer treatment procedures including mastectomy (CPT 19307) and craniotomy for tumor
(CPT 61510) require prior authorization. Aetna's oncology team reviews all cancer-related
prior-auth requests. Tier 1 members have access to Aetna's Centers of Excellence network
for complex oncological procedures. Coverage is 90% in-network for surgical oncology procedures.

PAGE 3
SECTION 3: ORTHOPEDIC AND MUSCULOSKELETAL
Total knee replacement (CPT 27447) requires prior authorization, documentation of
conservative treatment failure (minimum 3 months of physical therapy), and BMI documentation.
Tier 1 coverage is 85% in-network after $500 deductible. Physical therapy (CPT 97110)
is covered without prior authorization for the first 20 sessions per year.

PAGE 4
SECTION 4: GASTROINTESTINAL PROCEDURES
Upper GI endoscopy with biopsy (CPT 43239) and colonoscopy (CPT 45378) require prior
authorization when performed as elective screening outside standard intervals. Diagnostic
GI procedures ordered for acute symptoms do not require prior authorization. Coverage is
90% for Tier 1 members for medically necessary GI procedures.

PAGE 5
SECTION 5: FORMULARY AND DRUG COVERAGE
Specialty medications related to covered procedures are included in the Aetna Preferred
Formulary. Prior authorization for specialty drugs is processed by the Aetna Pharmacy team
separately from medical procedure prior-auth requests.
