Oscar Health Tier 3 — Digital Health Policy
Version 2024.1 | Effective January 1, 2024

PAGE 1
SECTION 1: DIGITAL-FIRST COVERAGE MODEL
Oscar Health operates a digital-first model with telehealth integration. Prior authorization
requests may be submitted digitally through the Oscar app or provider portal. Tier 3
standard review: 5 business days. URGENT: 24 hours. STAT: 4 hours via digital expedite.

PAGE 2
SECTION 2: TELEHEALTH AND VIRTUAL CARE
Oscar covers telehealth visits for non-emergency conditions at $0 copay. In-person visits
for conditions addressable via telehealth may require documentation of why telehealth was
insufficient. Mental health telehealth is covered at parity with in-person care.

PAGE 3
SECTION 3: COMPLEX PROCEDURE RESTRICTIONS
High-complexity procedures for Tier 3 Oscar members require prior authorization with
clinical review. Oncological surgeries (CPT 61510, 19307), cardiovascular bypass (CPT 33533),
and joint replacement (CPT 27447) require: prior authorization, specialist documentation,
and Oscar clinical team review. Coverage is 70% in-network after $3,000 deductible.

PAGE 4
SECTION 4: SPECIALTY MEDICATION COVERAGE
Specialty medications including biologics for inflammatory conditions and oncology agents
require prior authorization through Oscar's specialty pharmacy program. Step therapy is
required for biologic agents. Digital prior authorization tracking is available via app.

PAGE 5
SECTION 5: MEMBER ENGAGEMENT PROGRAM
Oscar offers incentives for preventive care engagement. Members earn rewards for completing
annual physicals, screenings, and health assessments. Diabetes prevention program participation
reduces cost-sharing for diabetes-related services by 20% for qualifying members.
